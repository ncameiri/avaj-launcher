Gang of Four (GoF) book

The term Gang of Four refers to four authors:

Erich Gamma

Richard Helm

Ralph Johnson

John Vlissides

In 1994, they published the book:

Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software

This book became the “pattern bible” for object-oriented programming.
It describes 23 classic design patterns, divided into 3 main categories:

Category	Purpose	Example Patterns
Creational	How objects are created	Singleton, Factory Method, Builder
Structural	How classes/objects are composed to form larger structures	Adapter, Decorator, Composite
Behavioral	How objects communicate and assign responsibilities	Observer, Strategy, Command
